Choose the area for your work, then use its sidebar to open the tool you need. Your role and enabled features determine what you can access.

## Choose an area

| Area | What you can find |
| --- | --- |
| Home | Your Mahallu’s overview and operation shortcuts. |
| Mahall | People, finance, accounting, registers, committee, services, events, announcements, programs, and reports. |
| Website | Public-site shortcuts and website-management previews. |
| Billing | Plan, payment-history, and usage previews. |
| Settings | General information, structure, finance configuration, notifications, users, roles, and activity. |
| Control Panel | Platform-wide workspace management, accounts, roles, plans, analytics, settings, and audit. |
| Public website and member area | Mahallu information, member sign-in, and the member dashboard. |

## Open a page

1. Choose the primary area, such as **Mahall** or **Settings**.
2. Expand a sidebar group and choose the page you need.
3. Use the breadcrumb above a record to identify its location and return to its parent page.
4. On a record page, choose a tab to review a different section or follow a linked family, house, or member.

You can collapse the desktop sidebar. On smaller screens, open the navigation menu to choose a page. Division names follow the terminology configured by your Mahallu.

## Search and quick actions

Enter text in the search control and choose a matching result from the grouped results. Use quick actions to open a common task directly.

A directory’s own search and filters narrow its records separately from the main search. Some directories search only the records already loaded; check the directory guide when a record is missing.

## Notifications

Open the notification bell to review the available counts and links. Choose an item to open its destination.

The dues notification currently points to an unavailable page. Use [Finance overview](/overview) or [Collections](/collection) to review available finance information.

## Sign out and inactivity

Open the account menu and choose the sign-out action to return to the sign-in page.

When an inactivity warning appears, choose **Stay Logged In** to continue or **Log out now** to end the session. If the session expires, sign in again; the application may return you to the page you were using.

## If you cannot open something

- Check that you have signed in to the correct application area and Mahallu.
- Ask your administrator about a missing feature or an access-denied message. A visible menu item does not guarantee that you can perform every action on its page.
- Use the current navigation when an old bookmark has moved. Some older bookmarks automatically forward to the replacement screen.
- Platform operators can have access beyond a single Mahallu. Opening a Mahallu’s administrator site does not sign you in as another user.
- Form templates currently have no Control Panel sidebar entry. Platform operators can use the [Form templates guide](/control/forms) to understand that tool.
