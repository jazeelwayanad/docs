## User accounts and access

Create operator accounts and manage their identities, access, passwords, sessions, and status.

### Steps

1. Open **Users**. Search by name, email, or phone and filter by role, Mahallu, or status.
2. Choose **Create User**, enter full name, email, and initial password, and optionally add a phone number, platform role, or initial Mahallu assignment. Save the account.
3. Use a row’s edit action to update identity details. Use its platform-role and Mahallu-membership dialogs to grant or remove access.
4. Choose **Reset Password** to enter and confirm a new password. Select the session-termination option if existing sessions should end.
5. Open the account’s [Sessions](/control/user-sessions) or use the available session-termination action.
6. Use suspend or reactivate for account status. Confirm single or selected-account deletion when removing accounts.

### Things to know

A new password must be at least eight characters and match its confirmation. Editing profile details does not change credentials. Accounts are operator identities, distinct from citizen member records. Access changes and destructive actions depend on your platform privileges.
