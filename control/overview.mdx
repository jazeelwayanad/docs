## Control Panel overview

Review platform totals, commissioned Mahallu workspaces, and recent audit activity.

### Steps

1. Open **Control Panel** with a platform account.
2. Review **Fleet Command Center**, platform statistics, and commissioned Mahallu workspaces.
3. Choose a Mahallu to open its command center, or select a platform-management tool.
4. Review **Audit Stream** for recent activity.

### Things to know

**Platform Root Access Denied** means your account lacks the required platform access. The **Cluster Healthy** badge is a fixed label and does not confirm live system health.
