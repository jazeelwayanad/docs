## Workspace command center

Review a Mahallu workspace and manage its profile, features, status, billing, and operational views.

### Steps

1. Open a workspace from [Fleet & Mahalles](/control/tenants).
2. Choose **Overview & Profile** to review Mahallu identity, Masjid leadership, location, and office-bearer information. Use **Edit profile** to make permitted changes, then save.
3. Use **Mahall Admin Operations** to inspect the available operational lists. Open the public or administrator site where needed.
4. In **Feature Flags**, choose **Inherit**, **Force on**, or **Force off** for a module and save.
5. In **Plans & Billing**, manage subscriptions, invoices, and payments as described in [Workspace billing](/control/tenant-billing). Review **Audit Stream** for changes.
6. Use suspend or reactivate to change status. Permanent deletion requires entering the workspace’s confirmation slug.

### Things to know

**Mahalle Name** cannot be blank. The profile’s slug is read-only. **Inherit** uses the plan entitlement; the force options explicitly override it. Opening the administrator site does not sign you in as another person. Available operations still depend on your access. A missing workspace cannot be opened.
