## Control Panel sign-in

Sign in with an operator account that has platform access.

### Steps

1. Open the Control Panel sign-in page supplied by your platform team.
2. Enter **Email** and **Password**, then choose **Log in**.
3. After signing in, open the Control Panel starting page or your requested destination.

### Fields and choices

Complete the required fields and any additional details requested for your selections.

#### Login form

| Field or control | Guidance |
| --- | --- |
| **Email** | Required. you@example.com |
| **Password** | Required. •••••••• |

### Things to know

Signing in successfully does not grant platform privileges. Mahallu administrator roles and platform roles are separate.
