Sign in with a platform account that has access to this tool. Your role determines which changes you can make.

## Platform settings

Maintain platform identity, policy settings, and onboarding defaults.

### Steps

1. Open **System settings**.
2. Review **General Platform Identity** and update the name, root domain, support email, currency, or timezone as needed.
3. Review **Security & Session Policies** for timeout and session-count settings.
4. Review **Fleet & Onboarding Defaults** for trial and audit defaults.
5. Choose **Save Changes**, then review the refreshed values. Inspect telemetry and fleet/compliance summaries separately.

### Fields and choices

Complete the required fields and any additional details requested for your selections.

#### Platform settings

| Field or control | Guidance |
| --- | --- |
| **Platform Name** | Required. Enter platform name. |
| **Root Domain** | Required. Enter root domain. |
| **Platform Support Email** | Required. Enter an email address. |
| **Default Currency** | INR (₹) - Indian Rupee, USD ($) - US Dollar, AED (د.إ) - UAE Dirham, SAR (﷼) - Saudi Riyal |
| **Platform Timezone** | Asia/Kolkata (IST), Asia/Dubai (GST), Asia/Riyadh (AST), UTC |
| **Session Idle Timeout (Minutes)** | 15 minutes (High Security), 30 minutes (Standard), 60 minutes (1 hour), 4 hours, 24 hours (1 day) |
| **Max Concurrent Device Sessions** | 3 active sessions, 5 active sessions (Recommended), 10 active sessions, Unlimited |
| **Require two-factor authentication for superadmins** | Enter require two-factor authentication for superadmins. |
| **Default Trial Duration (Days)** | Minimum 0; Maximum 90 |
| **Audit Stream Retention (Days)** | 90 Days, 180 Days (6 Months), 365 Days (1 Year), 730 Days (2 Years) |
| **Auto approve tenants** | Enter auto approve tenants. |
| **Allow public registration** | Enter allow public registration. |

### Things to know

The telemetry and fleet/compliance panels are read-only. Saving settings stores their values, but some runtime policy integrations remain incomplete. Changing the root-domain field does not connect or reconfigure a domain.
