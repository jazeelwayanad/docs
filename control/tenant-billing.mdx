## Workspace billing

Manage a Mahallu workspace’s subscription, invoices, and payment records.

### Steps

1. Open the workspace’s billing view or **Plans & Billing** in its command center.
2. In **Subscription**, choose the plan and status, then save the change.
3. To issue an invoice, enter an amount, optional description, and due date, then save.
4. Use **Mark paid** or **Void** on the appropriate invoice when needed.
5. To record a payment, enter its amount and method, add an optional reference, and save.
6. Review the subscription, invoice, and payment lists after each action.

### Fields and choices

Complete the required fields and any additional details requested for your selections.

| Field or control | Guidance |

#### Record payment

| Field or control | Guidance |
| --- | --- |

| **Reference (optional)** | UTR / cheque no. |

### Things to know

Invoice status changes and payment recording are separate. Recording a payment does not automatically settle an invoice. These tools manage saved billing records; the administrator Billing area currently shows previews.
