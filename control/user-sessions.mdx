Sign in with a platform account that has access to this tool. Your role determines which changes you can make.

## User sessions

Inspect and revoke the sign-in sessions associated with an operator account.

### Steps

1. Open an account’s sessions from [User accounts and access](/control/users).
2. Check the target user and review the session information shown.
3. Choose **Revoke** on the session you want to end.
4. Review the refreshed session list.

### Things to know

**No sessions on record** is the empty state. Revoking a session does not delete the account or remove its Mahallu memberships.
