## Workspace analytics

Review growth, subscription information, and metrics for a single Mahallu workspace.

### Steps

1. Open analytics for a selected workspace from its platform-management views.
2. Review the workspace heading, **Member growth** chart, subscription information, and supporting measures.
3. Use the back or workspace links to return to its command center.

### Things to know

This is a read-only view. Use the workspace command center or billing tools to change records. A missing or unavailable workspace cannot be opened.
