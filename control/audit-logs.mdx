Sign in with a platform account that has access to this tool. Your role determines which changes you can make.

## Platform audit log

Search platform audit activity and inspect individual entries.

### Steps

1. Open **Audit Logs & Compliance Stream**.
2. Enter a search or choose an action-category or Mahallu filter. Changing the filters returns you to the first page.
3. Use pagination to browse matching entries and open a row to inspect its details.
4. Choose **Export JSON** to download the currently loaded entries.

### Fields and choices

Complete the required fields and any additional details requested for your selections.

#### Audit logs explorer

| Field or control | Guidance |
| --- | --- |
| **Search** | Search by action, actor email, IP address… |
| **Action category** | Choose an available option. |
| **Mahallu workspace** | All Mahalles |

### Things to know

Export includes the current page rather than the entire matching history. The audit stream cannot edit, undo, or delete records. An empty result may mean no entries match the selected filters.
