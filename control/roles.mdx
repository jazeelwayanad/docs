Sign in with a platform account that has access to this tool. Your role determines which changes you can make.

## Platform roles

Grant and revoke platform-wide access for operator accounts.

### Steps

1. Open **Platform Roles & Permissions**.
2. Review the role summaries and current staff assignments.
3. Choose **Assign Role**, select the account, and choose **SUPER_ADMIN**, **PLATFORM_STAFF**, or **PLATFORM_SUPPORT**.
4. Choose **Grant Platform Access** and review the refreshed assignments.
5. Use **Revoke** to remove an account’s platform role.

### Fields and choices

Complete the required fields and any additional details requested for your selections.

#### Roles permissions

| Field or control | Guidance |
| --- | --- |
| **Select User** | Required. Choose a user... |
| **Platform Role** | Required. SUPER_ADMIN (Full Root Privileges), PLATFORM_STAFF (Operations & Billing), PLATFORM_SUPPORT (Support Mode Only); Default: PLATFORM_STAFF |

### Things to know

Platform roles control cross-workspace operations. They do not replace Mahallu roles. Your own platform privileges determine whether you can grant or revoke access; role summaries are not an editable permission matrix.
