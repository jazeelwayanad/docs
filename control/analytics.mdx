## Platform analytics

Review platform growth, subscriptions, and feature adoption.

### Steps

1. Open **Platform Analytics**.
2. Review the summaries, **Mahalle growth**, **Subscriptions**, and **Module adoption** panels.
3. Use platform navigation to investigate a workspace or plan in more detail.

### Things to know

This page is read-only. Viewing platform analytics does not by itself grant access to private records inside a Mahallu.
