## Platform analytics

Host: `control.<ROOT_DOMAIN>` · Route: `/analytics`

### Purpose and access

Review platform-wide growth, subscriptions, and module adoption.

You need an account session and platform access. Platform privileges are separate from tenant roles; privileged writes remain subject to endpoint role checks.

### Screen layout

The screen shows analytics summaries, Mahalle growth, Subscriptions, and Module adoption panels.

### Fields and displayed information


This screen has no editable fields or filters in its rendered components.


### Actions


Navigation and row interactions are described in the sequence below.


### Interaction sequence

Open Platform Analytics → inspect the charts and distributions → follow platform navigation to investigate tenants or plans.

### States and rules

This screen is read-only. Analytics requests use platform authorization; a chart does not grant access to a tenant’s private operational records.

## Platform analytics

Host: `control.<ROOT_DOMAIN>` · Route: `/analytics`

### ഉദ്ദേശ്യവും പ്രവേശനവും

Platform മുഴുവൻ growth, subscriptions, module adoption പരിശോധിക്കുക.

Account sessionഉം platform accessഉം വേണം. Platform privileges tenant rolesൽ നിന്ന് വേറെയാണ്; privileged writes endpoint role checksന് വിധേയമാണ്.

### സ്ക്രീൻ ക്രമം

Analytics summaries, Mahalle growth, Subscriptions, Module adoption വിഭാഗങ്ങളുണ്ട്.

### ഫീൽഡുകളും പ്രദർശിപ്പിക്കുന്ന വിവരങ്ങളും
