Sign in with a platform account that has access to this tool. Your role determines which changes you can make.

## Plans and included features

Create subscription plans and choose the features included in each plan.

### Steps

1. Open **Plans** and choose **New plan**.
2. Enter the plan name, key, price, and monthly or yearly billing period, then create it.
3. Choose **Features** on a plan and select its included features.
4. Choose **Save entitlements** and review the updated plan.
5. Choose **Remove** and confirm when deleting a plan.

### Fields and choices

Complete the required fields and any additional details requested for your selections.

#### Create plan

| Field or control | Guidance |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | Required. Growth |
| **Key** | Required. growth |
| **Price (leave blank for custom/contact-us pricing)** | 999.00; Minimum 0 |
| **Billing period** | Monthly, Yearly; Default: Monthly |

#### Plan features

| Field or control | Guidance |
| --- | --- |
| **Included feature** | Select or clear this option. |

### Things to know

Plan features combine with individual workspace overrides to determine availability. The plans managed here are saved records, separate from the fixed plan examples shown in administrator billing.
