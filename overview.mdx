## Finance overview

Review balances, collections, expenses, and outstanding dues before recording finance activity.

### Steps

1. Open **Finance**.
2. Review the cash balance, bank balances, and six-month income and expense chart.
3. Inspect **Recent Collections** and **Pending Dues & Arrears**.
4. Open [Collections](/collection) or [Payments](/payments) to record or manage a transaction.

### Things to know

A balance card summarizes recorded activity; it does not reconcile a bank account. If a panel is unavailable, check the related finance list before interpreting its figures.
