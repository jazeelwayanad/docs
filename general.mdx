Sign in to the selected Mahallu with access to this area. If access is denied, ask your administrator to review your role and enabled features.

## Website overview

Review the website-management screen and open your Mahallu’s public website.

### Steps

1. Open **Website**.
2. Review the displayed website address and status, then choose **View website** to open the supplied address.
3. Explore the password and launch controls only as a preview of the settings screen.

### Fields and choices

Complete the required fields and any additional details requested for your selections.

#### Website overview

| Field or control | Guidance |
| --- | --- |
| **Site password** | Enter new site passcode |

### Things to know

Password protection, **Save Password**, and launch controls do not currently save or enforce changes on the public website. The displayed address uses a preset domain pattern, so confirm the correct public address with your administrator.
