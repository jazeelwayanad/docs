## Marketing homepage

Host: `<ROOT_DOMAIN>` · Route: `/`

### Purpose and access

Introduce the platform and route visitors to signup, administrator login, member-demo access, or the control panel.

Entry, public access, and redirect rules are described for this screen below.

### Screen layout

The landing page has navigation, a hero, feature descriptions, tenant-isolation explanation, plan cards, calls to action, and footer. Signed-in visitors see account-aware navigation.

### Fields and displayed information


This screen has no editable fields or filters in its rendered components.

Choose a signup/onboarding call to action → open the configured setup route. Admin Login opens the administrator host; tenant/demo and control links open their respective surfaces.

### States and rules

Marketing claims and plan cards are static copy rather than evidence that every advertised feature works. Treat pricing here as source display text, not an authoritative live commercial plan.

Host: `<ROOT_DOMAIN>` · Route: `/`

### ഉദ്ദേശ്യവും പ്രവേശനവും

Platform പരിചയപ്പെടുത്തി signup, admin login, member demo, control panel എന്നിവയിലേക്ക് സന്ദർശകരെ നയിക്കുക.
