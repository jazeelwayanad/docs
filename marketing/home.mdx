## Explore Mahallu

Learn about Mahallu and choose the correct starting point for your role.

### Steps

1. Open the Mahallu platform homepage.
2. Review the feature descriptions and plan cards.
3. Choose a signup or onboarding action to create a Mahallu.
4. Choose **Admin Login** to manage an existing Mahallu, or the member/demo and Control Panel links for the corresponding area.

### Things to know

Signed-in visitors may see different account navigation. The feature descriptions and prices are static marketing information. Confirm plan pricing and feature availability with the platform team before making a subscription decision.
