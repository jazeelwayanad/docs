## Create a Mahallu with your account

Create a Mahallu workspace using an existing signed-in operator account.

### Steps

1. Open the **Create your Mahalle** form. Sign in if prompted.
2. Enter **Mahalle name**. Review the address name generated from it, or edit the address name manually.
3. Wait for the availability check and choose an available address.
4. Submit the form to create the workspace and continue to its administrator area.

### Fields and choices

Complete the required fields and any additional details requested for your selections.

#### Mahallu name

| Field or control | Guidance |
| --- | --- |
| **Mahalle name** | Required. Al Noor Mahalle |

#### Mahallu address

| Field or control | Guidance |
| --- | --- |
| **Mahallu address name** | Required. your-mahalle |

### Things to know

This shorter form is separate from [Mahallu setup](/mahallu-setup), which collects location and new-account details.
