## Subscription plan

Review the subscription-plan preview shown in the administrator billing area.

### Steps

1. Open **Billing**.
2. Review the displayed plan, renewal information, and **Plan Limits**.
3. Choose **Upgrade** to inspect **Growth Mahall** and **Juma Enterprise** in **Upgrade Subscription Plan**.
4. If you choose **Confirm Upgrade**, the screen displays a temporary confirmation and closes the dialog.

### Things to know

Plan names, prices, limits, and renewal information are example display values. The upgrade confirmation does not change your subscription or take payment. Ask the platform team to confirm or change your actual plan.
