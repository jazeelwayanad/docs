## Families

Register households and maintain their dwelling, contact, and welfare information.

### Steps

1. Open **Families** and choose **Add Family**.
2. In **1. Location & Housing**, select the configured division if used, and enter the house and address information.
3. In **2. Househead (Applicant)**, enter the househead’s name and primary mobile number. Add identity, education, employment, and health information where applicable.
4. In **3. Family Members**, choose **Add Member** for each additional person. Enter a name and relationship, then complete the available details.
5. Choose **Save & Register Family**. After a successful registration, the family profile opens.
6. To find an existing household, search by family, house, or head; use the house filter, sorting, and page-size controls. Open **View Family** or **Edit Family** from its actions.

### Additional details

Use **Student** or **Worker / Adult** for the applicant and additional members to display the relevant education or employment fields. Health and welfare choices reveal their associated details. Add or remove skill, qualification, and member rows before submitting. When editing a household, **1. House & Location** and **2. Emergency & Welfare** separate dwelling details from emergency contacts and support information.

### Things to know

The househead name and primary phone are required. Each additional member needs a name. Registration saves the house, family, and people in stages. If it fails partway through, check for records already created before retrying. The directory initially loads up to 100 families. Bulk **Assign House**, bulk deletion, **Import Families CSV**, **Batch House Map**, and **Census Summary** do not currently complete their advertised changes. Use individual record editing where available. **Export** downloads the records included by the selected export action.
