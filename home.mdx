Sign in to the selected Mahallu with access to this area. If access is denied, ask your administrator to review your role and enabled features.

## Home dashboard

Review your Mahallu at a glance and open the tools you use each day.

### Steps

1. Open **Home** after signing in.
2. Review your Mahallu identity, headline figures, charts, and setup milestones.
3. Search the operation shortcuts and choose the area you need. **Open Mahall Hub** opens the operational dashboard.
4. Use the website card to open the public site or its management area.

### Fields and choices

Complete the required fields and any additional details requested for your selections.

#### Dashboard modules

| Field or control | Guidance |
| --- | --- |
| **Search** | Search operations... |

#### Dashboard chart

| Field or control | Guidance |
| --- | --- |
| **Timeframe** | Weekly, Monthly, Yearly; Default: Weekly |

### Things to know

Some summaries may be unavailable when information cannot be loaded. A missing figure does not confirm that there are no records. The **Manage Treasury** shortcut and closing-cash link currently lead to unavailable pages; use **Finance** and **Accountant** navigation instead.
