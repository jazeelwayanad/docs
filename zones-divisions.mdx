## Divisions and areas

Find the families and members in your Mahallu’s configured subdivisions.

### Steps

1. Open the subdivision directory in **People**. Its name follows your configured division term.
2. Search by division name or code and review the family and member counts.
3. Open a division to inspect its roster.
4. Choose **Add family** to start household registration with that division selected.
5. To change subdivision definitions, open [Structure settings](/admin/structure-settings).

### Fields and choices

Complete the required fields and any additional details requested for your selections.

#### Divisions registry

| Field or control | Guidance |
| --- | --- |
| **Search** | Enter search. |

### Additional details

The **Add family** dialog uses the same three-step intake described in [Families](/family). It saves the house, household, and members in stages; check for already-created records before retrying after an error.

### Things to know

Your Mahallu may use a term such as ward, zone, or division. Divisions must be enabled in structure settings. New-family intake requires the househead’s name and primary phone number.
