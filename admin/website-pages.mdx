## Website pages

Review the public-page shortcuts offered in website management.

### Steps

1. Open **Website** → **Pages**.
2. Review the rows for **Home**, **Display Wall / Prayer Screen**, **Announcements & Circulars**, **Schedules & Prayer Times**, **Office Bearers & Committee**, and **Donations & Community Aid**.
3. Choose a row to open its supplied public address in a new tab.

### Things to know

This screen does not edit page content. The public home page is available; the display wall, announcements, schedules, committee, and donation shortcuts currently lead to unavailable pages. Member sign-in and the member dashboard are documented separately.
