## Health and support

Review member care needs and household welfare information.

### Steps

1. Open **Health and support** with an account allowed to view health information.
2. Choose **Active Welfare Aid**, **Chronic Illness & Medicine**, **Disability Register**, **Care & Bedridden**, or **Welfare Households**.
3. Search for a person or household and apply the available condition filters.
4. Open the linked member or family profile to update health or welfare information.

### Fields and choices

Complete the required fields and any additional details requested for your selections.

#### Health support

| Field or control | Guidance |
| --- | --- |
| **Search** | Search health or welfare cases... |

### Things to know

**Restricted Access** means your account cannot view these records. Member and household lists show separate empty messages and initially include up to 100 records each. New information is entered through member or household profiles.
