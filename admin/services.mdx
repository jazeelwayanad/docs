## Services and aid requests

Log service and aid requests, review them, and record their outcomes.

### Steps

1. Open **Services** and complete **Log a request**. Enter **Request type**, **Requester name**, and **Subject**, plus phone, linked member, and description if applicable.
2. Submit the request and select it in the list.
3. Choose **Start review** when available. Review its details and enter **Resolution notes** where appropriate.
4. Choose **Approve** or **Reject** according to the outcome.
5. Use **Mark completed** where offered after the work is complete. Filter by status to follow up on outstanding requests.

### Fields and choices

Complete the required fields and any additional details requested for your selections.

#### Record details

| Field or control | Guidance |
| --- | --- |
| **Request type** | Required. Enter request type. |
| **Requester name** | Required. Enter requester name. |
| **Requester phone** | Enter requester phone. |
| **Member** | Choose an available option. |
| **Subject** | Required. Enter subject. |
| **Description** | Enter additional details. |

#### Service requests table

| Field or control | Guidance |
| --- | --- |
| **Status filter** | All statuses |
| **Resolution notes** | Enter additional details. |

### Things to know

Available actions depend on the request’s current status and your access. Rejection and completion can include resolution notes. Logging requests here is separate from the currently unavailable request buttons on the member dashboard.
