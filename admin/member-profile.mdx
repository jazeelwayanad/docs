## Member profile

Review one member’s identity, household, education, employment, health, and residency details.

### Steps

1. Open a person from [Members](/members).
2. Review the profile sections. Reveal the identification number only when you need to read it.
3. Choose **Edit member** to update the record. A full name is required.
4. If no family is linked, choose **Link a family** and save the association. Follow the household link to review the family.

### Additional details

Choose **Student** to enter course and institution details, or **Worker / Adult** for employment information. Disability, chronic-illness, caregiver, mental-health, welfare, expatriate, and guardian details appear when you select the corresponding condition. Use **Add Skill** and the education-history controls to add entries; remove an entry before saving if it is no longer needed. **Use family address** copies the household address when a linked family is available. The same editing form is described in [Members](/members).

### Things to know

A missing or unavailable member cannot be opened. **No health details recorded** means no health information is available in that panel. Revealing an identification number only changes what you can see.
