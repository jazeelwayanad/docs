## Meetings and minutes

Schedule committee meetings and record their decisions and minutes.

### Steps

1. Open **Meetings and minutes**, then choose **Schedule meeting**.
2. Enter the meeting details and save.
3. Select the meeting from the list to view its details.
4. Add a decision or minute using the selected meeting’s form, then save.
5. Use **Edit** or **Remove** for the selected meeting; confirm removal when prompted.

### Fields and choices

Complete the required fields and any additional details requested for your selections.

#### Committee meeting form

| Field or control | Guidance |
| --- | --- |
| **Title** | Required. Enter title. |
| **Date** | Required. Choose a date. |
| **Status** | Scheduled, Completed, Cancelled |
| **Location** | Enter location. |
| **Agenda** | Enter additional details. |
| **Minutes** | Enter additional details. |
| **Attendees** | Select or clear this option. |

#### Committee decision form

| Field or control | Guidance |
| --- | --- |
| **Description** | Record a decision… |

### Things to know

Creating a meeting does not also create its decisions. Save meeting details and each decision separately. An empty meeting list means there are no meetings to select.
