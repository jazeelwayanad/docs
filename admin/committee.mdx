## Committee office bearers

Maintain committee posts and the people appointed to them.

### Steps

1. Open **Committee** and choose **Add post**.
2. Select a member and enter the designation, display order, and term dates.
3. Save the post and review the refreshed roster.
4. Use **Edit** to change an appointment. To delete a post, choose **Remove** and confirm.

### Fields and choices

Complete the required fields and any additional details requested for your selections.

#### Committee post form

| Field or control | Guidance |
| --- | --- |
| **Member** | Required. Select a member |
| **Designation** | Required. Enter designation. |
| **Display order** | Minimum 0 |
| **Term start** | Choose a date. |
| **Term end** | Choose a date. |

### Things to know

Committee appointments do not grant administrator or platform permissions. Manage operator access through [Users and roles](/admin/users-roles).
