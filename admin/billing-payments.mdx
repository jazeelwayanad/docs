## Billing payment history

Review the invoice-preview tools in the administrator billing area.

### Steps

1. Open **Billing** → **Payment histories**.
2. Choose **Download** beside an invoice row to open its preview.
3. Choose **Print or Save PDF** to open the browser print dialog. Select a printer or PDF destination and complete the browser action.

### Things to know

The rows use example billing information rather than your actual subscription payment history. Confirm invoices and payments with the platform team before using this preview as a financial record.
