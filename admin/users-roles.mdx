Sign in to the selected Mahallu with access to this area. If access is denied, ask your administrator to review your role and enabled features.

## Mahallu users

Give existing operator accounts access to this Mahallu and manage their assigned roles.

### Steps

1. Open **Settings** → **Users**.
2. In **Add a user**, enter an existing account’s email address and select a role.
3. Choose **Add** and review the refreshed list.
4. Change a listed account’s role using its role selector.
5. Choose **Remove** and confirm to remove its Mahallu access.

### Fields and choices

Complete the required fields and any additional details requested for your selections.

#### Admins table

| Field or control | Guidance |
| --- | --- |
| **Assigned role** | Choose an available option. |
| **Email** | Required. person@example.com |
| **Role** | Choose an available option. |

### Things to know

This list manages operator accounts, not citizen members. Your role determines which accounts and roles you can manage. Removing access does not delete the citizen member record.

## Mahallu roles

Create and maintain permission sets for operators within one Mahallu.

### Steps

1. Open the roles page from **Settings** → **Users and roles**.
2. Choose **Create role**, enter its identity, and select the required permissions.
3. Save and review the refreshed role list.
4. Use **Edit** to change a role or **Duplicate** to create another based on it.
5. Use **Deactivate** or **Reactivate** to change its availability.

### Fields and choices

Complete the required fields and any additional details requested for your selections.

#### Role form

| Field or control | Guidance |
| --- | --- |
| **Role name** | Required. Enter role name. |
| **Description** | Enter additional details. |

### Things to know

Built-in roles and access-level restrictions can prevent some changes. Deactivating a role does not delete an account. These roles apply within a Mahallu; [Platform roles](/control/roles) govern Control Panel access.
