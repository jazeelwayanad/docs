## Record a collection

Record incoming money and produce a receipt for the payer.

### Steps

1. Open **Collections** and choose **Record Collection**.
2. Choose **Collection Head / Category**. The category determines which payer, family, member, and additional fields appear.
3. Select the required family or member, or enter the payer’s details for a public collection. Selecting a family narrows the member choices.
4. Enter a positive **Amount (₹)**, choose **Payment Method**, and set **Receipt Date**. Add the billing period, category details, and remarks if applicable.
5. Choose **Record Collection & Issue Receipt**. After success, use **Print / Share Receipt** to open the receipt.
6. Choose **Print** to print or save as PDF, or **WhatsApp** to open sharing. Choose **Record Another** for a fresh entry.

### Fields and choices

Complete the required fields and any additional details requested for your selections.

### Additional details

Additional category fields can include text, dates, numbers, amounts, choice lists, checkboxes, and member or family selectors. Their labels, choices, order, and starting values depend on the category. Disabled fields are hidden.

### Things to know

The payment method starts at **Cash**. Complete category-specific information even where the form does not catch every missing value. **Cancel & Return** leaves without submitting. Enter moves to the next field except in multiline notes and on submission controls. Quick-add account and category dialogs save separately from the collection: enter a name and account type for an account, or a parent account and category name for a category. File uploads are not supported in additional category fields.
