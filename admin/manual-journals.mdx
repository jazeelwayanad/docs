## Manual journals

Record balanced accounting adjustments using debit and credit lines.

### Steps

1. Open **Manual journals** and choose **New Journal Entry**.
2. Enter the journal date, reference, and description.
3. Select ledger accounts and enter debit or credit amounts on at least two lines. Use **Add Line** for additional lines.
4. Check that total debits equal total credits, then submit.
5. Review the refreshed journal list.

### Fields and choices

Complete the required fields and any additional details requested for your selections.

#### Journal

| Field or control | Guidance |
| --- | --- |
| **Date** | Required. Choose a date. |
| **Reference / Cheque #** | e.g. JV-001 |
| **Narration / Description** | Required. Description of journal entry... |
| **Account** | Required. Choose an available option. |
| **Debit** | Debit (₹) |
| **Credit** | Credit (₹) |

### Things to know

An unbalanced journal is rejected. Lines with zero amounts are ignored when submitting. Account choices come from your [Chart of accounts](/charts-of-accounts).
