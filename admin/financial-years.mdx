## Financial years

Create accounting periods and manage which year is current, open, or closed.

### Steps

1. Open **Financial years** and choose **Add Financial Year**.
2. Enter **Financial Year Name**, **Start Date**, and **End Date**.
3. Submit and check the new period in the table.
4. Use **Set Current** on the year you want to select. Use **Close FY** or **Reopen** to change its status.

### Fields and choices

Complete the required fields and any additional details requested for your selections.

#### Financial year details

| Field or control | Guidance |
| --- | --- |
| **Financial Year Name** | Required. e.g. FY 2027-28 |
| **Start Date** | Required. Choose a date. |
| **End Date** | Required. Choose a date. |

### Things to know

All three creation fields are required. Review dates and any error shown when saving. Changing the current year and closing a year are separate actions.
