## Division profile

Review the households and residents assigned to one division.

### Steps

1. Open a division from [Divisions and areas](/zones-divisions).
2. Switch between the family and member roster tabs. Search within the selected roster.
3. Choose **Add family** to register a new household in this division.
4. Choose **Assign family** to open the assignment dialog, select an existing household, and save.

### Fields and choices

Complete the required fields and any additional details requested for your selections.

#### Division detail

| Field or control | Guidance |
| --- | --- |
| **Search** | Enter search. |

### Additional details

The **Add family** dialog uses the same three-step intake described in [Families](/family). It saves the house, household, and members in stages; check for already-created records before retrying after an error.

### Things to know

Switching roster tabs changes the search context. If existing families cannot be loaded, review the error before attempting assignment. A division that is missing or unavailable cannot be opened.
