<Note>Domain controls are previews. They do not connect or activate a domain.</Note>

Sign in to the selected Mahallu with access to this area. If access is denied, ask your administrator to review your role and enabled features.

## Website domains

Review the subdomain and custom-domain setup dialogs.

### Steps

1. Open **Website** → **Domains**.
2. Choose the subdomain edit action to review the current name. Enter a preview value and choose **Save**.
3. Choose **Connect Custom Domain** to open the domain dialog. Review its domain field and displayed connection instruction.

### Fields and choices

Complete the required fields and any additional details requested for your selections.

#### Website domains

| Field or control | Guidance |
| --- | --- |
| **Subdomain** | subdomain |
| **Custom domain** | e.g. www.kambalakkadmosque.com |

### Things to know

These dialogs only change the current screen. **Save Domain** does not connect a domain, verify ownership, or make a website available at that address. Subdomain preview names are normalized to lowercase letters, digits, and hyphens. Ask the platform team about an actual domain connection.
