## Waqf and property register

Maintain Waqf and property records for your Mahallu.

### Steps

1. Open the register from **Registers**, then choose **Add record**.
2. Complete the record details below, including the required names and dates.
3. Choose **Add Record**. After saving, the dialog closes and the list refreshes.
4. Use the row’s edit control to open an existing record, change its details, and choose **Save Changes**.
5. To delete a record, choose its removal control and confirm **Remove**.

### Fields and choices

Complete the required fields and any additional details requested for your selections.

#### Record details

| Field or control | Guidance |
| --- | --- |
| **Type** | Required. Waqf, Owned asset, Rented out |
| **Name/description** | Required. Enter name/description. |
| **Location** | Enter location. |
| **Area details** | Enter area details. |
| **Lessee name** | Enter lessee name. |
| **Lessee phone** | Enter lessee phone. |
| **Rent amount** | Enter rent amount. |
| **Acquisition date** | Choose a date. |
| **Remarks** | Enter additional details. |

### Things to know

The list initially shows up to 100 records and has no further-page controls. Complete the marked fields and review any validation errors returned when saving. This register does not offer certificate issuance.
