## Marriage register

Maintain marriage records for your Mahallu.

### Steps

1. Open the register from **Registers**, then choose **Add record**.
2. Complete the record details below, including the required names and dates.
3. Choose **Add Record**. After saving, the dialog closes and the list refreshes.
4. Use the row’s edit control to open an existing record, change its details, and choose **Save Changes**.
5. To delete a record, choose its removal control and confirm **Remove**.
6. To issue a certificate, choose **Issue** on the saved record. Once a number is assigned, choose **Print / Save Certificate** to open the preview, then **Print / Save PDF**.

### Fields and choices

Complete the required fields and any additional details requested for your selections.

#### Record details

| Field or control | Guidance |
| --- | --- |
| **Groom's name** | Required. Enter groom's name. |
| **Groom's father's name** | Enter groom's father's name. |
| **Bride's name** | Required. Enter bride's name. |
| **Bride's father's name** | Enter bride's father's name. |
| **Marriage date** | Required. Choose a date. |
| **Place** | Enter place. |
| **Officiant (Khazi/Imam)** | Enter officiant (khazi/imam). |
| **Witness 1** | Enter witness 1. |
| **Witness 2** | Enter witness 2. |
| **Mahr details** | Enter mahr details. |
| **Remarks** | Enter additional details. |

### Things to know

The list initially shows up to 100 records and has no further-page controls. Complete the marked fields and review any validation errors returned when saving. Certificate issuance is separate from saving the register entry. You can choose a PDF destination in the browser print dialog.
