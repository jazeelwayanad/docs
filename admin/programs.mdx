Sign in to the selected Mahallu with access to this area. If access is denied, ask your administrator to review your role and enabled features.

## Programs

Keep a list of Mahallu programs and their descriptions.

### Steps

1. Open **Programs**. Enter **Name** and an optional description in **Add a program**.
2. Choose **Add Record** and review the refreshed list.
3. Use the page controls to browse older entries.
4. To delete a program, choose **Remove** and confirm.

### Fields and choices

Complete the required fields and any additional details requested for your selections.

#### Record details

| Field or control | Guidance |
| --- | --- |
| **Program name** | Required. Enter program name. |
| **Description** | Enter additional details. |

### Things to know

Name is required. The list shows 20 programs per page. Programs do not currently have a separate detail page.
