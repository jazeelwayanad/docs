Sign in to the selected Mahallu with access to this area. If access is denied, ask your administrator to review your role and enabled features.

## Website visibility

Explore the visibility switches shown for website content and navigation.

### Steps

1. Open **Website** → **Visibility**.
2. Review **Page Visibility**, **Navbar Visibility**, and **Footer Visibility**.
3. Toggle an item to see its visible or hidden setting in the current screen.

### Things to know

These switches do not save or affect the public website. Reloading restores the default settings.
