## House profile

View a dwelling’s details and the family and members linked to it.

### Steps

1. Open a house from [Houses](/admin/houses).
2. Review **House Information**, including its division and address.
3. Open the linked family or member to review that record.
4. Use the edit-house control to change the dwelling details, then save.

### Fields and choices

Complete the required fields and any additional details requested for your selections.

#### House details

| Field or control | Guidance |
| --- | --- |
| **House number** | Required. e.g. 14/A |
| **House name** | Enter house name. |
| **Division** | No division |
| **Address** | Enter address. |
| **Notes** | Enter additional details. |

### Things to know

The page shows separate messages for missing family and member links. **History** currently shows **No history to show yet**; a house timeline is unavailable. A missing or unavailable house cannot be opened.
