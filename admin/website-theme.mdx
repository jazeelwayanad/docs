Sign in to the selected Mahallu with access to this area. If access is denied, ask your administrator to review your role and enabled features.

## Website theme

Explore the color and branding preview for a Mahallu website.

### Steps

1. Open **Website** → **Theme**.
2. In **Color Palette**, choose the primary, secondary, accent, and background colors.
3. Review the immediate change in **Live Theme Preview**.
4. Use **Reset** to restore the defaults. Review the media controls in **Branding & Media**.

### Things to know

This is a preview only. Colors do not save or change the public site. Media controls display example filenames and do not upload a logo or image.
