Sign in to the selected Mahallu with access to this area. If access is denied, ask your administrator to review your role and enabled features.

## Activity log

Review who changed Mahallu records and when.

### Steps

1. Open **Settings** → **Activity log**.
2. Review the newest entries, grouped by day. Each entry includes the actor, action, affected record, and time.
3. Use the page links to read older activity.

### Things to know

The list shows 30 entries per page. **No activity yet** is different from a denied-access message. This page is read-only and cannot undo changes or delete activity entries.
