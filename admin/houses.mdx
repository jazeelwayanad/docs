## Houses directory

Maintain physical dwellings independently from their families and residents.

### Steps

1. Open **Houses**. Search by number, name, or address, or filter by division and status.
2. Choose **Add house** and enter **House number**. Add a house name, division, address, and notes if needed.
3. Choose **Add House**. A successful save closes the dialog and refreshes the list.
4. Open a house to view its linked household and members. Use the row edit action to change its details and choose **Save Changes**.
5. Use **Deactivate** or **Reactivate** to change a house’s active status.

### Fields and choices

Complete the required fields and any additional details requested for your selections.

#### Search and filters

| Field or control | Guidance |
| --- | --- |
| **Search** | Search house number, name, address… |
| **Division filter** | All divisions |
| **Status filter** | Active, Inactive, All; Default: active |

#### House details

| Field or control | Guidance |
| --- | --- |
| **House number** | Required. e.g. 14/A |
| **House name** | Enter house name. |
| **Division** | No division |
| **Address** | Enter address. |
| **Notes** | Enter additional details. |

### Things to know

The status filter starts at **Active**. Switch to **Inactive** or **All** to find deactivated houses. If saving fails, review the inline error and correct the form. Empty search results do not necessarily mean the directory is empty.
