Sign in to the selected Mahallu with access to this area. If access is denied, ask your administrator to review your role and enabled features.

## Website analytics

Review the website analytics panels and their current availability.

### Steps

1. Open **Website** → **Analytics**.
2. Review **Traffic trend**, **Top Pages**, **Referrers**, **Countries**, **Devices**, and **Browsers**.
3. Choose **Refresh** to see the refresh indicator.

### Things to know

Live website analytics are not connected. The panels remain empty, and **Refresh** does not load traffic measurements.
