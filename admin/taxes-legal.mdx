## Taxes and legal tracker

Track filing deadlines and record when a compliance item has been filed.

### Steps

1. Open **Taxes and legal tracker** and choose **Add Statutory Compliance**.
2. Enter the title and due date. Add filing type, period, amount, and notes if applicable.
3. Save the item and review it in the list.
4. After completing the filing outside the application, choose **Mark Filed** to update its status.

### Fields and choices

Complete the required fields and any additional details requested for your selections.

#### Taxes legal

| Field or control | Guidance |
| --- | --- |
| **Filing Title** | Required. Enter filing title |
| **Type** | Required. Enter type. |
| **Due Date** | Required. Choose a date. |
| **Period** | Enter period |
| **Filing Fees / Tax (₹)** | 0.00 |
| **Notes / Reference** | Registration number, acknowledgment details... |

### Things to know

Title and due date are required. **Mark Filed** records the current date with the filed status. It does not submit documents or payments to a government portal.
