Sign in to the selected Mahallu with access to this area. If access is denied, ask your administrator to review your role and enabled features.

## Community reports

Choose a community report based on the information recorded in member profiles.

### Steps

1. Open **Reports** in the Mahallu area.
2. Choose **Blood group report**, **Expatriate register**, or **Yatheem register**.
3. Review the selected report’s counts and member table.
4. To correct information, open the person in [Members](/members).

### Things to know

Community reports are separate from finance and accounting reports.

## Blood group report

Find members by their recorded blood group.

### Steps

1. Open the report from **Community reports**.
2. Review the summary counts and available grouping filters.
3. Search by name or phone number to narrow the loaded list.
4. Review the member, family, contact, and report-specific details. Update the underlying information through [Members](/members).

### Fields and choices

Complete the required fields and any additional details requested for your selections.

#### Blood group report

| Field or control | Guidance |
| --- | --- |
| **Group filter** | All blood groups |

#### Member report table

| Field or control | Guidance |
| --- | --- |
| **Search** | Search name or phone… |

### Things to know

**No records** and **No matches** describe different situations. Clear filters if a person is missing. Reports use a loaded subset of member information and do not offer a separate record-creation form.
