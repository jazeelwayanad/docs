## Plan limits and usage

Review the resource-usage preview displayed for your Mahallu.

### Steps

1. Open **Billing** → **Plan limits and usage**.
2. Review the storage and resource cards for divisions, members, programs and registers, users, messaging, and website views.
3. Choose **Refresh** or **Recalculate** to see the loading indicator.

### Things to know

The figures and limits are fixed examples. Refreshing does not measure usage or enforce quotas. **Resources** has no connected action.
