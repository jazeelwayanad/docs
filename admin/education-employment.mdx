## Education and employment

Find member education, employment, job-seeker, expatriate, and skill information.

### Steps

1. Open **Education and employment**.
2. Choose **All Profiles**, **Job Seekers Desk**, **Higher Ed & Students**, **Expatriates / NRIs**, or **Skills Bank**.
3. Search by person, skill, or job. In **Skills Bank**, choose a skill to narrow the results.
4. Open the linked member profile to update the underlying information.

### Fields and choices

Complete the required fields and any additional details requested for your selections.

| **Search** | Search member, skill, job... |

### Things to know

Filters apply to the loaded profiles. This page does not have a separate profile-entry form. If access is denied, ask an administrator to review your education and employment access.
