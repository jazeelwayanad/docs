## Structure settings

Configure division names, household numbering, and family categories.

### Steps

1. Open **Settings** → **Structure**.
2. In **Organization**, enable divisions if needed, choose the division term, numbering method, and prefix, then save.
3. Add or edit individual division definitions. Use the order controls to rearrange them and activation controls to change availability.
4. Enable family categories if needed, choose their displayed term, and save.
5. Add or edit the individual family-category definitions separately.

### Fields and choices

Complete the required fields and any additional details requested for your selections.

#### Structure settings

| Field or control | Guidance |
| --- | --- |
| **Enable divisions** | Switch divisions on or off. |
| **Division term** | e.g. Ward |
| **House number prefix** | e.g. MH |
| **Enable family categories** | Switch family categories on or off. |
| **Family category term** | e.g. Category |

#### Division form

| Field or control | Guidance |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | Required. Enter name. |
| **Division code** | e.g. KBD |
| **Description** | Enter additional details. |

#### Family category details

| Field or control | Guidance |
| --- | --- |
| **Status Name** | Required. e.g. Category A, Welfare Eligible, Zakat |
| **Code / Short Key** | For example, A. |
| **Badge Color** | Choose an available option. |
| **Description** | Optional criteria or notes for this status tier |

### Things to know

Organization settings, divisions, and category definitions save separately. With divisions disabled, numbering uses a single Mahallu-wide sequence. Prefixes are saved in uppercase. Your chosen division term is used in related screens.
