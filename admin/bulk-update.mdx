## Bulk update

Apply shared changes to selected finance categories, ledger accounts, or transactions.

### Steps

1. Open **Bulk update** and choose **Public Categories**, **Chart of Accounts**, or **Transactions**.
2. Search and filter the loaded records, then select the rows you want to change.
3. For categories, choose the target fund, scope, ledger mapping, or status. When mapping a ledger, use the option to post pending transactions if needed.
4. For accounts, choose the target type, parent, fund, or status. For transactions, choose collections or expenses and the target fund or category.
5. Run the update and inspect the results and refreshed records.

### Fields and choices

Complete the required fields and any additional details requested for your selections.

### Things to know

Records may be updated one at a time, so a failed operation can leave some changes saved. Check results before repeating an update. Transaction lists include up to 1,000 loaded collections and vouchers; do not assume they cover every historical record.
