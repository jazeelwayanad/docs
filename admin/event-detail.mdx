## Event detail

Manage attendance and review income and expenditure associated with an event.

### Steps

1. Open an event from [Events](/admin/events).
2. In **Registrations**, enter the participant’s name and optional phone number, then choose **Register**.
3. After saving, review the refreshed list. Use the attendance action on a saved participant to change attendance.
4. Confirm removal when deleting a registration.
5. Review **Income & expenditure** for linked financial records.

### Fields and choices

Complete the required fields and any additional details requested for your selections.

#### Event registrations table

| Field or control | Guidance |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | Participant name |
| **Phone** | Phone (optional) |
| **Attendance** | Select or clear this option. |

### Things to know

Registration and financial records are separate. Changing attendance does not record income or an expense. A missing or unavailable event cannot be opened.
