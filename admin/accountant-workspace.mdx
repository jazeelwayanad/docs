## Accountant workspace

Open ledger tools and accounting reports from one workspace.

### Steps

1. Open **Accountant**.
2. Review the available account, journal, and finance summaries.
3. Choose [Chart of accounts](/charts-of-accounts), [Manual journals](/admin/manual-journals), or an [Accounting report](/reports-2).

### Things to know

Some summaries can be unavailable when information cannot be loaded. Check the underlying records before treating an empty summary as an empty ledger.
