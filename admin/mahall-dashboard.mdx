## Mahallu dashboard

Check setup progress, recent members, treasury information, upcoming events, and demographic summaries.

### Steps

1. Open the **Mahall** dashboard.
2. Follow a setup-checklist item to complete the corresponding settings.
3. Review recent members and upcoming events, then open a linked record for more detail.
4. Return to the dashboard to review the updated information.

### Things to know

A panel may be unavailable when its information cannot be loaded. Check the relevant directory before treating an empty summary as confirmation that no records exist.
