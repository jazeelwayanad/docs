Sign in to the selected Mahallu with access to this area. If access is denied, ask your administrator to review your role and enabled features.

## Announcements

Post notices for everyone, committee members, or a selected division.

### Steps

1. Open **Announcements**. In **Post an announcement**, enter **Title** and **Body**.
2. Choose **Audience**: **Everyone**, **Committee only**, or **One division**.
3. If you choose **One division**, select **Division**. The selector is available when your Mahallu has divisions.
4. Choose **Add Record** to publish the announcement.
5. Review its title, publication status, and audience in the list. Use the page controls for older notices, or **Remove** and confirm to delete one.

### Fields and choices

Complete the required fields and any additional details requested for your selections.

#### Record details

| Field or control | Guidance |
| --- | --- |
| **Title** | Required. Enter title. |
| **Body** | Required. Enter additional details. |
| **Audience** | Everyone, Committee only, One division |
| **Division** | Enter division. |

### Things to know

Title and body are required. New announcements are published when created. The list shows 20 records per page. The division selection is only used for the **One division** audience.
