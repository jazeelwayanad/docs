## Payments

Record paid expenses and manage their vouchers.

### Steps

1. Open **Payments** and choose **Record Expense**.
2. Select an expense category and enter the payee, amount, payment method, and date.
3. Choose a bank account when applicable and add a description.
4. Submit the form to record a paid expense.
5. Search by payee, voucher, or description; filter by fund, posting status, expense account, or category.
6. Edit a row to update its voucher, or confirm individual or selected-row deletion.

### Things to know

Payee, amount, and date are required. The selected category supplies the fund and ledger mapping. This form records an expense as paid; it does not take it through a separate approval process. Review any error notification before retrying.
