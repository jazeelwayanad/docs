## Sign in and create an account

Sign in to manage your Mahallu, or create an administrator account before setting up a new Mahallu.

### Steps

1. Open the administrator sign-in page supplied by your Mahallu.
2. Enter your **Email** and **Password**, then choose **Log in**.
3. Correct any sign-in errors shown on the form. After signing in, you return to your requested page or the administrator starting page.

### Sign-in details

| Field | What you need |
| --- | --- |
| **Email** | Your operator account’s email address. |
| **Password** | Your operator account’s password. |

Both fields are required. Member phone-code sign-in is [a separate service](/member/login).

### Things to know

An existing signed-in account skips the login form. An inactivity or expired-session message means you need to sign in again.

## Admin registration

Create an account to operate the administrator application. An account is separate from a citizen member record.

### Steps

1. Choose **Sign up** on the sign-in page.
2. Enter your **Full name**, **Email**, **Password**, and **Confirm Password**.
3. Choose **Create account**. If you do not yet manage a Mahallu, continue to [Mahallu setup](/mahallu-setup).

### Fields and choices

Complete the required fields and any additional details requested for your selections.

#### Register form

| Field or control | Guidance |
| --- | --- |
| **Full name** | Required. Enter your full name |
| **Email** | Required. you@example.com |
| **Password** | Required. Create a password |
| **Confirm Password** | Required. Confirm your password |

### Things to know

Use the same password in both password fields. If registration fails, correct the message shown before submitting again.

## Where you go after signing in

The administrator starting page takes you to the appropriate next screen.

### Steps

1. Open the administrator application.
2. Sign in if prompted.
3. If your account already manages a Mahallu, the first associated Mahallu opens. If it has no associated Mahallu, the setup wizard opens.

### Things to know

This starting page does not offer a Mahallu-selection screen.
