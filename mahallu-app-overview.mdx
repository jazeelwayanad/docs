## Set up a new Mahallu

1. [Create an account and sign in](/getting-started).
2. Complete [Mahallu setup](/mahallu-setup).
3. Configure [divisions and family categories](/admin/structure-settings).
4. [Register families](/family) and [maintain member records](/members).
5. Configure [finance settings](/admin/finance-settings) before recording collections or expenses.
6. Give other operators the appropriate [users and roles](/admin/users-roles).

## Understand the main records

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Mahallu | The community workspace you manage. Some screens use the labels Mahalle or Mahall. |
| Member | A citizen record in your community. |
| User or account | A person’s sign-in identity for operating the application. |
| Family | A household unit whose members are linked together. |
| House | The physical dwelling linked to a household. |
| Division | A configured area such as a ward or zone. Your Mahallu chooses the term. |
| Mahallu role | The permissions an operator has inside a Mahallu. |
| Platform role | Access to platform-wide tools in Control Panel. |
| Operational fund | A grouping for community income and expenses. |
| Ledger account | An accounting record used for posting and reporting. |

## Access and feature availability

What you can open or change depends on your role and the features enabled for your Mahallu. If you see an access message, ask your administrator to review your permissions. Platform tools require separate platform access.

Some screens currently offer previews or unfinished controls. Their guides explain what you can use today and when an action does not save a change. In particular, confirm subscription details with the platform team, prayer times with your Masjid, and the actual reporting period before relying on report totals.
