## Members

Maintain individual citizen records, including family links, education, work, health, and residency information.

### Steps

1. Open **Members**, then choose **Add member**.
2. Enter a full name in the identity section and complete contact and family information.
3. Complete the education and work, health, and address tabs as applicable.
4. Save the form. The dialog closes and the directory refreshes after a successful save.
5. Search, use the summary filters, or filter by family and blood group to find a person. Adjust sorting and rows per page as needed.
6. Open a row to view its profile, or edit the saved record. Confirm individual deletion when removing a person.

### Fields and choices

Complete the required fields and any additional details requested for your selections.

### Additional details

Choose **Student** to enter course and institution details, or **Worker / Adult** for employment information. Disability, chronic-illness, caregiver, mental-health, welfare, expatriate, and guardian details appear when you select the corresponding condition. Use **Add Skill** and the education-history controls to add entries; remove an entry before saving if it is no longer needed. **Use family address** copies the household address when a linked family is available.

### Things to know

A full name is required. Searches and pagination work on the initially loaded list of up to 100 members. **Import Data**, **Bulk Photos**, and **ID Cards** currently show messages without completing those operations. Some bulk deletions can fail without a separate error for each person; check the list afterward. Bulk **Assign Family** currently displays a confirmation without saving the new family association. Use individual editing or a family profile to link people. **Export** downloads a CSV of the available records; the selected-row export includes the selected people.
