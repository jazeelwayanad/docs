Sign in to the selected Mahallu with access to this area. If access is denied, ask your administrator to review your role and enabled features.

## General settings

Review Mahallu and account information, then open the settings you need.

### Steps

1. Open **Settings** → **General**.
2. Review the Mahallu name, Masjid, workspace information, country, organization, and signed-in account role.
3. Review the modules grouped by category.
4. Use **Change** for organization to open structure settings, or **View plan** to open billing.
5. In **Configure**, choose structure, finance, notifications, users and roles, or activity.

### Things to know

Identity and account values are read-only on this page. **Not set** indicates an unavailable value. Module availability depends on your plan and platform configuration.
