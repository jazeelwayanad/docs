## Member sign-in

Use the phone number recorded with your Mahallu to access member services.

### Steps

1. Open your Mahallu’s public website and choose **Member Login (Phone + OTP)**.
2. Enter your registered mobile number and choose **Send code**.
3. When a code is available, enter the six digits and choose **Verify and log in**.
4. Use resend after the cooldown if needed, or **Use a different number** to return to phone entry.
5. After verification, your requested member page or dashboard opens.

### Fields and choices

Complete the required fields and any additional details requested for your selections.

#### Member otp login form

| Field or control | Guidance |
| --- | --- |
| **Phone number** | Required. +91 98765 43210 |
| **6-digit code** | Required. 000000 |

### Things to know

SMS delivery is not currently connected, so a member cannot rely on receiving a code by phone. Contact your Mahallu administrator if you cannot obtain a code. Codes expire after five minutes, allow five failed attempts, and can be used once. The send-code response does not confirm whether a phone number is registered. An expired or inactive session requires signing in again.
