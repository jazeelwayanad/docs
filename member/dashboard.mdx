## Member dashboard

Review your member profile and the community panels shown after sign-in.

### Steps

1. Sign in through [Member sign-in](/member/login).
2. Review **Member Profile**, including your name and any recorded phone or email.
3. Review **Friday Notices & Announcements** and **Community Events & Milad**.
4. Check **Citizen Services & Certificates** for the services displayed.

### Things to know

**Apply Online** and **Request Aid** do not currently submit applications. This includes **Marriage (Nikah) NOC**, **Mahallu Release (NOC)**, and **Welfare & Aid Request**. Contact the Mahallu office to request these services. The **Active** badge is a fixed display label, not a verified membership-status check. Community feeds may be empty because member access is not fully connected. If your session ends, sign in again.
