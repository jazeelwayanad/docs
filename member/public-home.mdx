## Your Mahallu website

Visit your Mahallu’s public page and find the member sign-in link.

### Steps

1. Open the public website address supplied by your Mahallu.
2. Review the Mahallu and Masjid identity, community information, notices, and upcoming events.
3. Choose **Member Login (Phone + OTP)** to sign in. If already signed in, choose **Go to Member Dashboard**.

### Things to know

Displayed prayer times are fixed examples, not a verified live schedule. Confirm prayer times with your Masjid. Service cards do not submit requests. Announcement and event feeds are not fully connected for public and member access, so empty panels do not confirm there are no notices or events.
